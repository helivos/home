Marketing

What it is for

Marketing is where you send a message to a group of people at a time you choose: an email about a new class, a text about a holiday timetable, an app notification about a challenge. It also holds your reusable templates and shows what is scheduled, drafted and sent.

For messages that send themselves when something happens to one person (a new lead, a member slipping), use Workflows instead.

Who can see it

Admins and managers only. The Marketing item does not appear for the Staff role. If a staff member opens the page directly, they see a message saying only admins and managers can send marketing.

How to use it

Send a campaign

  1. Open Marketing from the sidebar.
  2. Choose how to send it: Email, SMS (text) or App notification.
  3. Who would you like to email? Pick one or more lists and tags. Next to each you will see how many people will actually receive it. Only people who have opted in are counted.
  4. Which template? Pick a template, or Start from scratch.
  5. Write your message.
  6. Email. Write a subject and use the editor.
  7. SMS. Up to 1,600 characters.
  8. App notification. A title, and a message up to 240 characters.
  9. To send later, turn on scheduling and pick a date and time under Send at.
  10. Click Send Now, or Schedule. Or save it as a draft to finish later.

If a channel has not been connected yet (for example you have not set up texting), the button says Save as draft instead, and nothing is sent until it is connected.

Cancel or finish a campaign

On the Marketing page, the Scheduled section lists campaigns waiting to go. You can cancel one before it sends. Drafts can be opened and sent. Recent shows what went out and how many were sent.

Make a template

  1. On the Marketing page, find Templates.
  2. Choose the channel.
  3. Give it a name and, for email, a default subject.
  4. Paste your email's HTML, with {{content}} where each campaign's message should go and {{gym_name}} where your gym name should appear. The preview on the page shows how it will look.
  5. Save.

Who a campaign actually reaches

Helivos checks consent again at the moment of sending, never from earlier. That means someone who unsubscribes after you hit schedule will not receive it.

Audience Email SMS App notification
Subscribed members Members who agreed to email and have an address Members who agreed to texts and have a mobile Every active member with the app (no consent needed)
Leads Leads who agreed to email Leads who agreed to texts Not available. App notifications go to members, not leads
A tag Members with the tag who agreed Members with the tag who agreed Members with the tag

If no one in the chosen audience has agreed, Helivos will not send, and tells you "No one in this list has consented to this channel. Marketing is opt-in."

Marketing consent is the member's own choice. They can change it in their profile, by the unsubscribe link in any email, or by replying STOP (or START to turn it back on) to a text. Staff cannot change someone's consent for them.

What gets added automatically

You cannot remove these, and you should not try. They are required by the Spam Act 2003.

  • Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link in the footer, and your company address (set in Settings > General).
  • Every marketing text ends with "Reply STOP to unsubscribe".
  • Anyone who unsubscribes is remembered so they are not emailed again even if their account is later removed.

Tags and smart tags

Tags are labels you put on members, such as "Competition squad". You can use them as audiences.

A smart tag adds and removes itself based on a rule, and is rechecked every night. The rules available are:

  • Joined recently and never attended
  • Joined within the last N days
  • Has not attended for N days
  • Attended at least N classes in the last D days
  • Is at a risk level (Low, Medium, High, Non attender)
  • Is on a plan
  • Is on a trial
  • Is on hold

Smart tags are handy for audiences like "Haven't been in for 21 days" without keeping a list by hand. You create tags from a member's profile (Overview, tags). You cannot add or remove people from a smart tag manually, because the rule controls it.

FAQs

Why does the audience count look smaller than my member count? Only members who have opted in and have an email address (or mobile) are counted. App notifications count every active member.

Can I email people who have not opted in because it is about their membership? Not from Marketing. Account messages, such as a plan about to end, are sent by Workflows, which are allowed to go to everyone because the member needs to receive them.

Can I send marketing to someone who is on hold or cancelled? Members who are cancelled (inactive) are not included. Members on hold are included, if they have opted in.

How do I know it worked? The Recent list shows "sent / total" for each campaign. If the numbers differ, some messages failed or were skipped.

Where do I set my sender email and branding? Settings > Marketing (logo, header colour, sender email, and email domain setup).

Is there a character limit for emails? Email is rich text and has no practical limit for normal use. SMS is 1,600 characters. App notifications are 240.
