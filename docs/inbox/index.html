Inbox

What it is for

The Inbox is one place for everything coming in from people:

  • Leads. People who have enquired but have not joined yet.
  • Messages. Texts, in-app messages, and Instagram and Facebook messages from members and new leads.
  • Support. Your chat with the Helivos team (see also Support).

If the Inbox shows a number in brackets in the sidebar, for example Inbox (3), that is how many conversations have a message nobody on your team has opened yet.

Who can see it

The Leads tab needs the Leads permission. The Messages tab needs the Inbox permission. The Support tab is always there. If someone has neither Leads nor Inbox, the Inbox disappears from their sidebar. Admins and managers can switch these on and off per user in Settings > Users.

Leads

How leads arrive

A lead is created when someone:

  • fills in the enquiry form on your website,
  • is sent in from your own website by the Lead API (see Settings > Leads),
  • messages your Instagram or Facebook page and is not already a member, or
  • is added by hand by your team.

When a new lead arrives, the staff who have lead alerts switched on are notified.

How to add a lead by hand

  1. Open Inbox and go to the Leads tab.
  2. Click Add lead.
  3. Enter a name (required) and, if you have them, an email, phone and notes.
  4. Choose a status: New, Contact or Lost.
  5. Click Add Lead.

How to work a lead

  1. Click a lead to open it.
  2. Use Edit to update their details, notes or status.
  3. If they would like to try you out, click Convert to trial and choose the trial. They stay on the list as a lead while on a trial.
  4. When they are ready to join, click Convert to member, check their details, choose the plan you agreed, and confirm. Helivos creates the member and sends them an invitation to set up their account and payment.
  5. If someone is not going to join, set their status to Lost.

The Leads list clears itself each month

The list is meant to show who you are working on right now.

  • A lead disappears from the list the moment you convert them to a member.
  • At the start of each month, every lead from earlier months who is still New or Contacted is marked Lost and removed from the list.

Nothing is deleted. Lost leads stay in the Lost Leads report so your numbers are still right.

Filters

Use the tabs (All, New, Contact, Lost), the search box (name, email or phone), and the filters for source (where the lead came from) and added (last 7 or 30 days).

Messages

How to reply

  1. Open Inbox and go to the Messages tab.
  2. Choose a tab: All, New (a member wrote last and nobody has answered), Unread, or Archived.
  3. Click a conversation to open it. Opening it marks it as read.
  4. Type your reply. Replies can be up to 1,000 characters.
  5. Choose how to send it in the Send as menu.
  6. Click Send.

Send as: which options appear

Option When it is offered
A text Members only. Needs a mobile number.
An in-app message Members only.
Instagram If the person has messaged your Instagram.
Facebook If the person has messaged your Facebook page.

The menu starts on whichever channel they used last, so if they texted, your reply goes by text.

The 24 hour rule for Instagram and Facebook

Instagram and Facebook only let a business reply for a limited time after the person last wrote. Helivos shows "You can reply on Instagram until..." under the reply box. When the time runs out, the box is switched off, and you can reply again once they message you again. (If your gym has Meta's "Human Agent" permission, the window is 7 days instead of 24 hours.)

Other things you can do on a conversation

  • Mark unread. Puts it back as unread so you or a colleague come back to it.
  • Archive. Tucks it away. If the person writes again, it comes back automatically.
  • Already a member? (on a lead's conversation). If someone who is already a member messaged you from a new Instagram or Facebook account, link the conversation to their member profile. The messages move onto the member and the duplicate lead is removed.

Filters and search

Search by name, sort by latest, oldest or name, and filter by channel (text, app, Instagram, Facebook) or by who wrote last (customer or staff).

FAQs

A new person messaged us on Instagram. Where are they? They appear in Messages with a Lead badge, and also in Leads, with their source shown as Instagram. If they turn out to be a member, use Already a member? to merge them.

Why can I not reply to someone on Instagram? It has been too long since they last wrote. Meta blocks the reply. As soon as they send another message, you can answer.

Why is the text option missing? The person is not a member (a lead) or the conversation came in over Instagram or Facebook. Texts and in-app messages are for members.

I replied by text but the member says they did not get it. Check the member has a mobile number on their profile. The conversation header shows the number, or "No mobile number". If it looks right, tell the Helivos team so they can check the text provider.

Can two staff see the same conversation? Yes. Read and archive status is shared across the team, not per person.

Why did a lead I never touched move to Lost? Leads from earlier months that are still New or Contacted are marked Lost at the start of each month. See the Lost Leads report.

Do I lose the lead's history when I convert them? No. The conversation moves with them onto the member, and an activity log of changes is kept against the lead.
