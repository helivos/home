Retention

What it is for

Retention helps you keep members. It answers three questions every morning:

  1. Who should I contact today, and what should I say?
  2. Who has started to slip, and who has come back?
  3. How is every member doing, grouped by how likely they are to leave?

It does this by watching how often each member actually turns up to class, and comparing them with their own habit.

Who can see it

Admins, managers and staff, unless an admin has switched off the Retention permission for that user (Settings > Users). Opening a member's profile from this page also needs the Members permission.

How to use it

Work through "Reach out today"

  1. Open Retention from the sidebar.
  2. Look at the Reach out today card at the top. It lists up to 10 members, each with a reason in plain English, their score and risk level, and when you last got in touch.
  3. Read the suggested message. It is filled in with the member's first name and your gym name.
  4. Click Copy message, then paste it into a text, email or the Inbox and change it however you like. Reach out today does not send anything itself.
  5. After you have contacted them, click Log contact and choose called, messaged or emailed. The member leaves the list and is given a 5 day rest, so you are not chasing the same person twice.
  6. If now is not the right time, click Not now. That snoozes the member for your default snooze length for their risk level, and they stop appearing in retention lists until it ends.

Check what changed

  1. Scroll to Daily briefing.
  2. Read who moved into a higher risk level, who is improving, who joined, who bought a paid membership, and what actions staff logged yesterday.
  3. To look back, change the date. You can open any day in the last year (days before your first nightly score have no risk changes to show).

Look after new members

  1. The New members: first 30 days card shows everyone in their first month.
  2. Each row shows the day they are on, classes attended out of 10, and a pace label: No first class yet, Behind, On track or Reached target.
  3. The people who need the most attention are at the top.

Keep regulars happy

The Regulars to keep in touch with card lists members who come very often and who nobody has spoken to in a while. A quick hello goes a long way with your best members.

Browse everyone by risk

  1. Below those cards, members sit in columns: Low Risk, Medium Risk, High Risk, Non Attenders and Canceled.
  2. Click a column heading to show only that group. Click it again to show everyone.
  3. Use the search box to find a name, and the sort menu to order by name, join date or risk.
  4. Click a member to open their profile.
  5. If a percentage and an arrow show next to a name, see "Retention percentage" below.

Log what you did

Open a member's profile, go to the Retention tab (or use Log action on their Overview), and record a call, chat, email or other contact. Later, the system compares their risk level then and now, so you can see whether it helped.

Snooze someone

Some members are away on holiday or injured, and you do not want them flagged. On the Members page, use the snooze icon on their row.

  1. Click the snooze icon.
  2. Choose 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or until you turn it off. The first option is your default for their current risk level.
  3. They disappear from retention lists and reports until the snooze ends. To end it early, open the same menu and turn it off.

How it works, in plain English

The four risk levels

Every member gets a risk level, shown as a coloured label.

Level What it means
Low risk Keeping to their usual habit.
Medium risk Starting to slip. A good time for a friendly message.
High risk Well off their usual pattern. Reach out soon.
Non attender Has not been to a class for more than 30 days, or has never been.

The health score

Behind each level is a health score from 0 to 100. A higher score means a healthier habit.

The important idea is that each member is compared with themselves. Someone who trains once a week is not worried about after 8 days. Someone who normally comes every second day is. So the score looks at three things:

Part Question it asks Default share of the score
Recency How long since their last visit, compared with how often they usually come? 50%
Frequency Did they come as often in the last 30 days as in the 30 days before? 30%
Booking Do they have a future class booked? 20%

The three parts are blended into one score. Then the score decides the level:

  • 70 or above: Low risk
  • Above 33 and below 70: Medium risk
  • 33 or below: High risk
  • No visit in the last 30 days, or never visited: Non attender, whatever the score is

Your gym can change the 70 and 33 cut-offs, and turn each part up, down or off, in Settings > Retention.

A worked example

Sam usually trains every 3 days. It has been 6 days since their last visit. They came 12 times in the last 30 days and 15 times in the 30 days before. They have a class booked on Friday.

  1. Recency. Sam is 6 days out and their usual gap is 3 days, so they are 2 gaps overdue. Recency is full marks at 1 gap or less and falls to zero at 4 gaps. At 2 gaps it is 66.7 out of 100.
  2. Frequency. 12 visits now against 15 before is 12 / 15 = 80 out of 100.
  3. Booking. They have a class booked, so 100 out of 100.
  4. Score. (50% x 66.7) + (30% x 80) + (20% x 100) = 33.3 + 24 + 20 = 77.3. That is 70 or more, so Sam is Low risk.

Now imagine Sam cancels the Friday booking. Booking drops to 0 and the score becomes 57.3. Sam is now Medium risk, even though they have not missed anything yet. This is why a member's risk can change the moment they cancel a booking.

How the "usual gap" is worked out

Helivos looks at the days a member checked in over the last 90 days and finds the typical (median) number of days between visits. That gap is kept between 2 and 14 days. If the member has fewer than 3 visits in that time, Helivos assumes a gap of 7 days because there is not enough history to know their habit.

Retention percentage

By default members only show a risk level. If you turn on Show retention percentage in Settings > Retention, you will also see the health score as a percentage on the Retention page and on the member's profile.

On the Retention page you may also see a small arrow next to the percentage. An up arrow means the score has risen by 3 points or more over the last week. A down arrow means it has fallen by 3 or more. No arrow means it has been steady. The arrow only appears once there are two nightly scores at least 2 days apart.

Who is included

Members are left out of the Retention page, its lists, its reports and Reach out today when:

  • they are snoozed, or
  • their plan or trial has Include in retention switched off (set in Settings > Plans). Gyms use this for free, staff or complimentary plans.

Members on hold stay visible on the Retention page (marked "On hold") but are never put on the Reach out list, never nudged and not counted as absent, because their absence is expected.

Reach out today: what puts someone on the list

Each member appears once, under the first reason that applies. This is the order of importance:

Order Reason When it applies
1 Owed a reply Their most recent message in the Inbox is from them and you have not replied (and the chat is not archived).
2 Intro ending Their trial ends within the next 7 days.
3 Missed first class They joined at least a day ago and have not yet had a first class.
4 First slip Their risk level got worse overnight.
5 Coming less Low or Medium risk, but they came noticeably less in the last 30 days than the 30 days before.
6 Back on track Their risk level improved overnight to Low.
7 Congratulate They had their first class, or hit a milestone, today or yesterday.
8 Milestone They are close to their next milestone (within 5 classes by default).
9 Anniversary They joined on this date in an earlier year.
10 Birthday It is their birthday.
11 Check in A regular (see below) nobody has contacted lately.

Inside each reason, the members with the lowest health score come first, except where a more natural order exists (for example, the oldest unanswered message first, or the trial ending soonest first).

Anyone you contacted in the last 5 days (including by logging a contact) is skipped, except people who are waiting on a reply. The list shows 10 members by default, with a note like "Top 10 of 18" if there are more.

New member pace

A new member's 30 days start at their first checked-in class, or on the day they joined if they have not been yet. The target is 10 classes in 30 days.

  • Reached target: 10 or more classes.
  • No first class yet: no classes yet.
  • Behind: fewer than 60% of the classes you would expect by now if they were on an even pace to 10.
  • On track: everyone else.

For example, on day 15 you would expect 5 classes. 60% of 5 is 3, so a member with 2 classes is Behind and a member with 3 is On track. On day 20 you would expect about 6.7 classes, so 3 is Behind and 4 is On track.

Regulars to keep in touch with

A regular is someone with 12 or more check-ins in the last 28 days (about three a week) who has had no contact from you in 60 days (or never). They are listed with the most active first.

Keep an eye on, Congratulate, Milestones (on the Home dashboard)

The Home dashboard has a Retention tab that uses the same nightly scores:

  • Keep an eye on: members who moved from Low to Medium, members who moved from Low or Medium to High, and members who are still Medium.
  • Today's classes: Low and Medium risk members who are booked into a class today, so the coach can say hello.
  • Congratulate: first classes and milestones from today or yesterday.
  • Milestones coming up: members within a few classes of their next milestone.

Milestone numbers and "coming up within" are set in Settings > Retention. The defaults are 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400, 500, 750 and 1000 classes, and "within 5".

The motivation score

Alongside risk, each member has a motivation score. Members never see it. Staff see a coloured dot beside the member (yellow or red) when it gets low, and those members also appear in the dashboard's at-risk list even if they are still turning up.

The score goes up for good habits and down for bad signs:

Event Points
Trained in at least 1 different week +5 (once)
Trained in at least 4 different weeks +10 (once)
Trained in at least 8 different weeks +20 (once)
Trained in at least 26 different weeks +40 (once)
Goal set +5
Goal achieved +25
Referred a friend (entered by staff) +15
Social media mention (entered by staff) +10
Logged a lift or run in the app +2 (once a day per type)
Personal best +5 (once a day per type)
Booked a class and did not check in -10 each
No class for 14 days or more -15 (once per month while it stays true)
No app activity or class for 21 days -10 (once per month while it stays true)

A member turns yellow at -10 or lower and red at -30 or lower. Your gym can change those two numbers.

FAQs

Why is a member who comes every day showing as Medium risk? Their score is probably being pulled down by one of the three parts. The most common cause is no future class booked (the Booking part is 20 points). Open their profile and the Retention tab to see each part out of 100.

Why is a brand new member a Non attender? Anyone who has never checked in is a Non attender, because there is no habit to compare against. Once they attend, their level is worked out normally. The "Missed first class" reason in Reach out today exists for exactly this person.

Why does someone who attends weekly show Low risk after 8 days away, but another member is Medium after 5? Because the score is relative to each person's own habit. See the worked example above.

Why are there fewer members here than on the Members page? The Retention page leaves out snoozed members and members on plans or trials with "Include in retention" switched off.

Why is nobody on Reach out today? Either everyone has been contacted in the last 5 days, or there is genuinely nothing to act on. It is a good problem to have.

Do the numbers update live? The risk level and percentage on the Retention page and Members page are worked out when you open the page. The Daily briefing, "Keep an eye on" and "First slip" use a snapshot taken overnight, so changes show the next morning.

Why does the High risk count differ from "At Risk (Churn)" on the Home dashboard? They measure different things. High risk is based on check-ins and a health score. At Risk (Churn) counts paying members who have not booked a class within the churn window. See Home dashboard.

Can I stop the automatic text that goes to members who slip into High risk? Yes. Go to Workflows and switch off or edit the two "Nudge" workflows. See Workflows.

A member asked why they got a text. Why did they? They moved into High risk or Non attender since yesterday, they have a mobile number, and they agreed to marketing texts. Without consent no text is sent.

How do I know whether my follow-up worked? Log the action on their profile. After that their action list shows "Improved", "Same" or "Worse", comparing their risk level when you logged it with their level now. The Retention report summarises this for the month.
