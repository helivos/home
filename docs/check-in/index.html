Check In

What it is for

Check In is the screen you keep open at the front desk. It marks members as present at today's classes, and lets you add walk-ins who did not book.

It is small, but it is the most important page for your numbers. Every attendance figure in Helivos comes from check-ins: last visit, attended sessions, risk levels, the health score, milestones, the motivation score, absent clients, visits, no-shows and class fill. A class nobody is checked into counts as a class where everybody was a no-show.

Who can see it

Admins, managers and staff. It is not affected by the per-user permission switches.

How to use it

Check in a booked member

  1. Open Check In from the sidebar. It opens on today.
  2. Pick the class from the dropdown. Classes are listed in time order, and cancelled classes are left out.
  3. Under Booked, find the member and click Check in.
  4. They move to the Checked in list with a green badge.

Check in someone who did not book (a walk-in)

  1. Select the class they are attending.
  2. In the Check in box, type the member's name under Search for a member.
  3. Click Check in next to the right person.

This creates a booking and checks them in at the same time. People who are already booked in this class do not appear in the search, because they are in the Booked list already.

Check in on a different day

Use the date arrows at the top to go to another day, for example to catch up on yesterday. The label shows Today, or the date if you have moved away from today.

What you will see next to each name

Each person has a small status badge: Active, On Hold, Overdue or Cancelled. It is there so the person at the desk can see, for example, that someone is Overdue before they train. See Members for what each status means. The badge is information only. Helivos does not stop you from checking someone in.

What check-in changes

When you check someone in, Helivos updates their attended sessions count straight away. Everything else (risk level, health score, milestones and so on) is worked out from that, either live or in the nightly update. See Retention.

FAQs

A member turned up but is not in the Booked list. They did not book this class. Use the walk-in search to check them in. If they booked a different class, check in to that one or move them in Schedule.

The class I need is not in the dropdown. Either it is cancelled, or it is on a different day. Change the date, or check the class exists on the Schedule calendar.

I checked the wrong person in. Can I undo it? This page has no undo button. Contact Helivos support and tell them the member, class and date, and they can correct it.

It says "This session is fully booked" when I try to check a walk-in in. The class is at capacity (including people who booked but have not arrived). Staff walk-ins respect capacity. You can raise the capacity for that one session on the Schedule page using Capacity override.

Does checking someone in work if they have used all their sessions this week, or have not signed the waiver? Yes. When staff check someone in, the plan limits, trial limits and waiver are not enforced. Those rules apply when members book for themselves. If your gym wants stricter control at the door, use the status badge as your guide.

Do I need to check people in if I use the member app? Yes. Booking a class does not count as attending. Attendance only counts once someone is checked in.

Why does a member's "Last check-in" not match what I expect? It is the date of the last class they were checked into. Booked but not checked in does not count.
