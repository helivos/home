Schedule

What it is for

Schedule is where you build your timetable. You set up the kinds of class you run (class templates), put sessions on the calendar, assign coaches, and roster staff shifts. Members book into what you put here, and every attendance number in Helivos hangs off it.

Who can see it

Anyone with the Schedule permission. Admins and managers also see call-back settings under Settings > Schedule.

The words we use

  • Class template. A kind of class, for example "Strength" with a colour, a capacity of 20 and a description. It is the blueprint.
  • Session. One occurrence of a class on a date and time, with a coach. This is what members book.
  • Shift. A block of time a staff member is rostered on.

How to use it

Set up a class template

  1. Open Schedule, click the more menu (three dots) and choose Class templates.
  2. Click Add class template.
  3. Enter a name, a capacity (the number of spots), a colour, and a session type (Group or PT for personal training).
  4. Add a description. Members see it when they look at the class.
  5. Save. You can add a photo once the template exists.

Add sessions to the calendar

  1. Go back to the Calendar tab.
  2. Click Add session, or click a blank slot on the calendar.
  3. Choose the class type, the coaches, the date, the start time and the duration.
  4. Optionally set a capacity override if this one session should hold more or fewer people than the template, and add notes.
  5. Tick free if it is a session that should not count toward anyone's plan (for example a free community class).
  6. To repeat it, choose how often and until when. A repeating series can have up to 366 sessions.
  7. Save.

Coaches follow your shift roster. If someone is rostered at that time, they can be assigned automatically. You can tick or untick coaches to set them yourself. If nobody is rostered, Helivos reminds you to assign a coach.

Change or cancel a session

  1. Click the session on the calendar.
  2. Edit it, or cancel it.
  3. If it is part of a repeating series, you are asked whether the change is for just this session or the whole series.

You can also open check in from a session to check people in from here. See Check In.

Roster shifts

  1. Go to the Shifts tab.
  2. Click Add shift.
  3. Pick the staff member, date, start and end time, and add notes if needed.
  4. The shifts for the week are listed. Click one to edit it.

Shifts do two jobs. They decide who is offered as a coach for sessions at that time, and a rostered shift keeps that time from being offered for member call-backs (see below).

Booking conflicts

Members with the app can set up a standing weekly booking ("every Tuesday at 6am"). Helivos books them in for the next 4 weeks. If a session is full, or booking it would go over their weekly plan limit, Helivos cannot book it for them and raises a booking conflict.

When there are any, a Conflicts button appears at the top of the calendar. Open it and, for each one, choose:

  • Add anyway. Books them in regardless of capacity or limit.
  • Pick another session. Move them to a different session instead.

Call-backs

Settings > Schedule lets admins and managers choose the hours people can book a call-back from the app, how long each slot is, a buffer between calls, minimum notice, and how far ahead they can book. Staff with Call alerts switched on (Settings > Users) are told about each booking. A private calendar link lets a coach see their call-backs and classes in Google, Apple or Outlook calendar.

Rules that decide whether a member can book

When a member books for themselves, Helivos checks these, in this order, and stops at the first one that fails. Staff can add someone regardless using Add anyway or a walk-in, which skips the plan rules.

  1. The session is not cancelled.
  2. The member has a plan or trial, if your gym requires one (Settings > Member App > Require a plan or trial). Free sessions skip this.
  3. They have signed the liability waiver.
  4. Their plan includes this type of session (Group or PT).
  5. If they are on a trial with a booking cap, they have not used all of it.
  6. If their plan has a weekly limit, they have not used all of this week's sessions. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
  7. They do not already have a booking in this session.
  8. The session is not full.

Cancelled bookings, cancelled sessions and free sessions do not count toward a trial cap or weekly limit.

A worked example

Jo is on a "3 per week" plan. On Monday she books Tuesday's class and Wednesday's class. On Thursday she tries to book a third and a fourth session for the same week.

  • The third works: that is her 3rd session this week.
  • The fourth is refused with "You've used all 3 of your weekly sessions. This plan allows 3 classes per week."
  • If she cancels Tuesday's class, she can book another, because cancelled bookings do not count.
  • If the fourth session is marked free, she can book it, and it does not count against her.

FAQs

A member cannot book and says nothing happens. They will have seen a message with the reason. The most common are: unsigned waiver, no plan or trial, their plan not including that session type, a full session, or weekly limit reached. Check their profile for each.

How do I make one class bigger than usual? Edit that session and use Capacity override. The template stays unchanged.

How do I stop a class running on a public holiday? Cancel that session. If it is part of a series, choose just this session.

What does "free" mean on a session? It does not count toward a member's weekly limit or trial cap, and people without a plan can book it (if you require plans, free sessions are the exception).

Why does the calendar suggest a coach I did not pick? Coaches follow the shift roster. Untick them or tick someone else to override it.

What happens when a booking is cancelled? It frees the spot and stops counting toward the member's weekly limit or trial cap.

Where do the TV screens and class programmes live? The code for display screens exists but it is not in the Schedule tabs in this version, so it is not documented here.
