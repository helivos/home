Members

What it is for

Everyone signed up at your gym, in one list. From here you add members, see who owes money, find someone fast, and open a member's profile to manage their plan, billing, holds, notes and retention.

Who can see it

Admins, managers and staff with the Members permission. Some actions are limited:

Action Who
View, add and edit members Admin, manager, staff
Export all members to CSV Admin
Place a member on hold Admin always. Managers and staff only if allowed in Settings > Security
Charge a one-off fee Admin always. Managers and staff only if allowed in Settings > Security

How to use it

Find a member

  1. Open Members from the sidebar.
  2. Type a name, email or phone number into the search box.
  3. Use the filters for status (Active, On Hold, Overdue, Cancelled), plan, and waiver (Signed, Pending, Not sent, Declined).
  4. Use the sort menu to order by name, join date, or highest or lowest score.
  5. Switch between list and card views with the view toggle.

Add a member

  1. Click Add member.
  2. Fill in the Profile section: name and email are needed. Phone, date of birth, gender, goal and notes are optional.
  3. In the Billing section, choose a plan or a trial. A member cannot have both.
  4. Leave Send invite on if you want them to receive an email to set up their account.
  5. Optionally add body details (height, weight, target weight) and a home location.
  6. Click Add Member.

The sign-up date defaults to today. If you are entering someone who joined earlier, change it, because retention and reports use it.

Open a profile

Click a member. The profile has up to five tabs:

  • Overview. Details, waiver and consent, tags, goals and body details, notes and Log action buttons, and any hold requests waiting for you.
  • Billing. Their plan, renewal, payments, add a payment method, Charge fee, cancel membership, and cancellation notice.
  • History. A timeline of their bookings, notes, actions and changes.
  • Motivation. Their motivation score and the events behind it (if switched on).
  • Retention. Their risk level, health score broken into its three parts, a plain English summary of how they have been training, and a one-year risk history.

Put a member on hold

  1. Open their profile.
  2. In the hold section, enter a reason for hold (for example, travelling for two months) and confirm. You only see this if your role is allowed to place holds.
  3. Their billing is paused with Stripe, and they show as On Hold everywhere.
  4. When the hold has an end date, it lifts automatically that night and billing starts again. Otherwise you take them off hold by hand.

Members can also request a hold from the member app. Requests appear on their profile. You can Approve, Approve with extension (if you are allowed to change hold length), or Decline with an optional reason. Each plan sets its own limits (see Settings > Plans).

Cancel a membership

  1. Open their profile and go to Billing.
  2. Click Cancel membership.
  3. Pick a reason. This is required, and it feeds the Cancellations report.
  4. Choose when it takes effect, and confirm.

If a member has given notice, you will see the notice and an end date. Withdraw notice is available if they change their mind and the notice can still be withdrawn.

Snooze a member

Click the bell icon on their row, then choose 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or until you turn it off. Snoozed members are left out of the Retention page and retention reports. The length offered first is your default for their current risk level.

Import and export

  • Export all members to a CSV is an admin action.
  • Import from another system is in Settings > Your Data. Members imported with an email can be sent an invite as part of the import.

What the statuses mean

A member has one status at a time, and it comes from their billing, not their attendance.

Status When
Cancelled The account is no longer active.
On Hold A staff member placed them on hold (or an approved hold has started). This wins over everything else below.
Active They are on one of your trials, or their subscription is in good standing.
Overdue They owe money: their subscription is past due, unpaid or paused, they have a failed payment on record, or their subscription was cancelled or ran past its period end without renewing.

Note: status (billing) and risk (attendance) move independently. A member can be Active and High risk, or Overdue and Low risk.

FAQs

Why is a member Overdue when they paid yesterday? A member shows Overdue while any of their payments is still recorded as failed. Helivos keeps one record per invoice, so when a retry or a manual payment succeeds on that invoice it flips from failed to paid and the flag clears. If you collected the money some other way (cash, for example), the invoice may still read as failed. Open their Billing tab to see exactly which payment is flagged.

Why can I not see Export? Only admins can export.

What is the difference between Cancelled and Lost? Cancelled is a member whose membership ended. Lost is a lead who never joined.

Why is a member's Motivation dot yellow? Their motivation score has dropped to your yellow level (default -10). Typical causes are missed booked classes and weeks with no visit. See Retention.

I added a member but they cannot book a class. They need a signed waiver, and a plan or a trial (if your gym requires one). Check the Waiver field on the Overview tab and their plan. See Schedule for the full booking rules.

Can staff change a member's email or plan? Staff can edit most details. Plan changes and cancellations are on the Billing tab.

What happens to a member's data when they are deleted? Deleting a member removes their login and personal data, including messages and thread state. It asks for your password (and authenticator code if MFA is on) first because it cannot be undone.
