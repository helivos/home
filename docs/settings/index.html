Settings

What it is for

Settings is where you set up how Helivos behaves for your gym: your details, who has access, your plans and policies, how retention is measured, and which services are connected. Most changes here affect the whole gym, so it is worth reading the notes under each tab before changing something.

Who can see it

Anyone with the Settings permission can open it, but which tabs they see depends on their role.

General

Your gym's basic details.

  • Company name and company address. The address is used at the bottom of marketing emails, which the law requires.
  • Region. Country is Australia and is fixed once payments are connected. Choose your timezone and currency. Set the timezone to where your gym is, because times shown across the app, such as message times in the Inbox, use it.
  • Company logo. Shown in the sidebar and on your branded pages.
  • Resources. Links to Support, What's new, Getting started (admins), and the Terms and Privacy Policy.

Billing

This is your gym's own subscription with Helivos: which plan you are on, the price, and when it renews. To change your plan, click through to Support and ask. Additional recurring items you pay for appear under Subscriptions.

Users

Where you manage your team.

Add a user

  1. Click to add a new user. Admins and managers can do this. Only an admin can create another admin.
  2. Enter their name and email, and choose a role: Admin, Manager or Staff.
  3. Set their alerts (see below).
  4. Save.
  5. For a new person, an admin can then send them a password reset so they can set their own password.

Only admins can disable, re-enable or delete a user. Staff can see the team list but not change it.

Roles

Role Can do
Admin Everything, including plans, integrations, security, data import and export.
Manager Everything day to day, plus Reports and Marketing. Cannot see Plans, Integrations, Security or Your Data.
Staff Members, Inbox, Retention, Check In and Schedule. No Reports, no Marketing, no revenue figures.

You can rename the three roles under Role labels if your gym uses different titles.

Switch off areas for one person

Open a user and use the permission switches to turn off Leads, Members, Retention, Inbox, Workflows, Schedule, Reports or Settings. A switched-off area disappears from their sidebar. Admins can never be restricted. Reports and Marketing stay limited to admins and managers regardless of these switches.

Alerts

Each user can choose whether they get notified of new leads, support replies and call-back bookings, and whether alerts come in the app, by email, or both.

Locations

Your gym sites. A member can be given a home location when you add them. Add and edit locations here.

Schedule

Admins and managers only. Sets the hours people can book a call-back from the app, how long each call is, a buffer between calls, how much notice you need, and how far ahead people can book. It also gives each coach a private calendar link so their call-backs and classes appear in Google, Apple or Outlook calendar. See Schedule.

Policies

The documents members see. Leave a box blank to use Helivos's standard wording.

  • Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
  • Membership Hold Policy, shown on the hold request screen in the app
  • Liability Waiver, written in markdown. Leave blank to remove the waiver question altogether.
  • Agreement and Trial Agreement (only admins can edit)

Members who sign up have to sign the waiver before they can book classes. See Members.

Marketing

How your emails look and who they come from: email logo, header colour, sender email, and the email domain authentication setup (including a DMARC record) that helps your emails reach inboxes instead of spam. See Marketing.

Retention

This tab controls how Helivos decides who is at risk. It is the most important tab for your numbers, so it is explained in full below.

Retention thresholds

Four settings for alerts and the At Risk (Churn) card. See Home dashboard.

Setting Default What it does
On-hold member count 5 Alert when this many members are on hold.
Money owed revenue risk (%) 0 (off) Alert when money owed reaches this share of paying revenue.
Minimum monthly revenue (%) 0 (off) Alert when paying revenue falls below this share of total possible revenue.
Churn risk window (days) 14 How long without a booking before a paying member counts as At Risk (Churn).

Show retention percentage

Off by default. Turn it on to show each member's health score (0 to 100%) next to their risk level. See Retention.

Health score

Three sliders, one each for Recency, Frequency and Booking. Each slider has five stops: Off, Low, Normal, High, Max.

  • Normal is the standard weight (Recency 50, Frequency 30, Booking 20).
  • Low halves a part's weight, High makes it one and a half times, Max doubles it, Off removes it.
  • The three are then rescaled to add up to 100, so the score always runs from 0 to 100.

Example. Set Recency to Max, leave Frequency on Normal, and set Booking to Off. The weights become Recency 77%, Frequency 23%, Booking 0%. Now only how recently someone came and how often they have been coming matters. A booked class no longer protects them from a worse level.

Two numbers set the cut-offs:

  • Low risk starts at (default 70). A score at or above this is Low risk.
  • High risk ends at (default 33). A score at or below this is High risk. Anything in between is Medium.

Someone who has not visited for more than 30 days (or never) is a Non attender whatever their score is.

Before you save, Helivos shows a preview: how many members would change risk level, for example "12 members would change risk level. Low 80 to 71, Medium 30 to 38, High 10 to 11". Look at it before you save, because changing these moves people between levels and can trigger automatic nudges. You cannot save if Low risk does not start above where High risk ends, or if every part is off.

Snooze defaults

How many days a snooze lasts by default at each risk level (1 to 365). Non attenders use the High risk number. Staff can still pick a different length when snoozing.

Milestones

The class counts worth celebrating, separated by commas (for example 50, 100, 300), and how close a member must be to their next milestone before they appear under Milestones coming up.

Cancellation reasons (admins)

The list of reasons staff choose from when cancelling a membership. They feed the Cancellations report.

Custom fields

Extra fields you can add to member profiles.

Plans (admins)

Your membership plans, trials and add-ons. Plans here are what you assign to members and what sets their rules.

A plan

Field What it does
Plan name and Price What the member is charged.
Billed How often: weekly, monthly, yearly and so on.
Sessions per week The most classes a member can book in a week. Blank means unlimited.
Session types Whether the plan covers Group classes, PT, or both.
Plan length (weeks) How long the plan runs. Blank means ongoing. Ongoing plans send no ending notices unless the member cancels.
Default payment method Bank direct debit or card.
Notice to cancel (days) How much notice a member must give. Default 30.
Hold length (weeks) How long every hold on this plan lasts. Default 2. The member only picks the start date.
Hold weeks per year The most hold weeks in a calendar year. Default 4. With 2-week holds that is two holds.
Holds per year How many holds a member may request in a year. Blank means no limit.
Days between holds How long after a hold ends before they can request another. 0 means no wait.
Rate List, Corporate or O.G.
Ending notices How many days before the plan ends to send the first notice, reminder and final notice. See Workflows.
Include in retention Untick for free, staff or complimentary plans so those members are left out of retention lists, reports and nudges.

You can also switch the member app's tools (Lift, Track and others) on or off per plan.

A trial

A trial has a name, a session cap (how many classes it includes), a length in days, its own ending notices, and an Include in retention setting.

Add-ons

Extras a member can add on top of a plan.

Member notice

How long a member's notice period is, how soon billing details are removed after they give notice, what happens to signed agreements and waivers after removal, and how long payment records are kept (blank keeps them). Check the payment records period with your accountant before you set it.

Leads (admins)

Creates the Lead API keys that let your own website send enquiries straight into the Inbox. A key is shown once when you create it, and can be revoked at any time. Keep keys on your website's server, never in code that runs in a visitor's browser. Enquiries sent this way count as website leads in your reports.

Integrations (admins)

Connect the services Helivos works with.

  • Stripe. Needed for members to pay. Until Stripe is connected, members cannot check out. You can connect a new Stripe account or use one you already have.
  • Meta Ads and Google Ads. Syncs ad spend and leads into the Monthly Performance Report. For Google, choose which conversion actions count as a lead.
  • Instagram and Facebook messages. Brings messages from your page into the Inbox. You must be an admin of the Facebook page.
  • ClassPass. Coming soon.

Security (admins)

  • Two factor authentication. Turn on to require two-step verification at every staff login.
  • Editable profile fields. Which member profile fields users with the Edit Profile permission can change.
  • Logs. An activity log for your whole space, filterable by member and date range.
  • Extra charges. Who can apply a one-off charge (for example a no-show fee) from a member's profile.
  • Placing members on hold. Who can place a member on hold.
  • Changing a hold when approving. Who can change a hold's length, or allow more holds than the plan permits, when approving a request.

Admins can always do all of these. For the three "who can" settings, you choose whether Managers and Staff can too.

Member App

How the member app looks and works.

  • Carousel. The slides on the home screen. Reorder them with the arrows.
  • Accent colour. Your brand colour in the app.
  • Booking. Three switches.
  • Show spots left to members. Off hides the count but members still see when a class is full. Staff always see it.
  • Allow repeat bookings. Off stops members setting up a weekly repeat. Repeats they already have are paused and resume if you turn it back on.
  • Require a plan or trial. On means people who sign up without a plan or trial cannot book or use performance tools, and are asked to speak to a coach. Members you have already set up keep full access, and staff are never affected.
  • Performance tools. Switch off the tools your gym does not use. They disappear from the app for everyone, and if every tool is off the Performance tab is hidden.

Your Data (admins)

  • Backup. Your data is backed up daily.
  • Import. Upload a CSV from another system, then map its columns onto Helivos fields. Rows with problems are reported by row so you can fix them and re-upload.
  • Export. Download complete CSV files, with optional filters.

FAQs

I changed the health score sliders and now my numbers moved. Can I undo it? Yes. Put the sliders and cut-offs back (the defaults are Normal, Normal, Normal, 70 and 33) and save. Scores are recalculated, so levels move back too.

I turned on "Require a plan or trial" and a member says they cannot book. That is the setting working. Set the member up on a plan or trial (Members, then their Billing tab), and they can book. Free sessions are exempt.

I cannot see the Plans or Security tab. Those are admin only. Ask an admin on your team.

Where do I change someone's role? Settings > Users, open the person, change Role, and save.

Why does my timezone matter? Times shown in the app, such as when a message arrived, are displayed in your gym's timezone. If it is wrong, times will look off by a few hours.

How do I stop automatic nudge texts? In Workflows, not Settings. See Workflows.

How do I turn off two-step verification for one person? You cannot. It is all or nothing for the gym.
