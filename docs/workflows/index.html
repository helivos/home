Workflows

What it is for

Workflows are messages that send themselves when something happens. A new lead arrives, a member starts slipping, a trial is about to end. You write the message once, choose how it goes out (email, text, app notification), and Helivos sends it at the right moment, every time.

Who can see it

Anyone with the Workflows permission can open the page and see what has been sent. Managers and admins can create and change workflows. Staff can look but not edit.

How to use it

Read the list

  1. Open Workflows from the sidebar.
  2. Each row shows the name, the channels used (for example "Email + Text"), any follow-ups, the trigger (what starts it), and what happened in the last 30 days, such as "12 sent, 2 skipped, 1 waiting".
  3. Use the tabs (All, Active, Draft, Paused) and the Type and Channel filters to narrow the list.

Switch a workflow on or off

Use the status menu on the row.

  • Active. Sending.
  • Draft. Written but not sending yet. The welcome message for new leads starts as a draft.
  • Paused. Switched off for now. Nothing sends, and you can switch it back on any time.

Change a message

  1. Click the workflow.
  2. Edit the Subject (used as the email subject and the app notification title) and the Message.
  3. Use merge fields in curly brackets to personalise it. The form shows which ones are available for that workflow. The common ones are {first_name}, {name} and {gym}.
  4. Check the Preview with sample values at the bottom.
  5. Save.

Create your own workflow

  1. Click New workflow.
  2. Give it a name and choose When it should run.
  3. Choose which channels to use.
  4. Choose when to send: straight away, or a set time after the trigger.
  5. Write the subject and message.
  6. Optionally add up to 4 follow-up messages, each sent a set time after the one before.
  7. Save, then set the status to Active.

The triggers

Trigger Who gets it Type of message
A new lead arrives The lead Marketing
A member slips into a risk level (High risk, or Non attender) The member Marketing
A member's plan is about to end (first notice, reminder, final) The member Account
A member's trial is about to end (first notice, reminder, final, 1 class left, all classes used) The member Account

Every gym starts with a ready-made set: a welcome for new leads (off until you switch it on), two "Nudge" workflows for members who slip, and the plan and trial ending notices (on). You can edit and switch these off but not delete them.

Marketing vs account messages

This matters because of the law and because it decides who actually receives the message.

  • Marketing messages (new lead welcome, nudges) are only sent by email or text to people who agreed to receive marketing on that channel. Without consent the message is skipped, and the list shows it as skipped. Texts automatically end with "Reply STOP to opt out" unless your message already says stop.
  • Account messages (plan or trial ending) are sent whatever the marketing consent says, because the member needs to know.
  • App notifications are not subject to marketing consent, but only go to members who have the app installed.

Consent can only be changed by the member (in their profile, from an unsubscribe link, or by replying STOP or START to a text). Staff cannot change it.

How the timing works

Ending notices

For plans and trials, you decide how many days before the end date each notice goes out set in Settings > Plans on each plan and trial. New plans start at 14, 7 and 1 days before the end. New trials start at 3 days and 1 day, with no reminder. A blank turns that notice off.

  • A plan's end date is the date the subscription is set to end, or if there is none, the start date plus the plan's length in weeks. Plans with no end never send an ending notice.
  • A trial's end date is the day it started plus the trial's length in days.
  • Trials can also notify when one class is left and when all classes are used.
  • Each notice is only sent once for each end date.

Nudges for members who slip

Every night, after Helivos works out each member's risk level, it checks who has just moved into High risk or become a Non attender. Those members get the nudge, once. It is not sent again every night they stay there. Members on hold are skipped. See Retention for how risk levels work.

Follow-ups

A follow-up goes out a set time after the message before it, unless the person has moved on:

  • For a new lead, it stops if the lead's status is no longer "New" (someone followed them up).
  • For a member who slipped, it stops if they replied to you, came back to a class, or their risk level changed.
  • If the person has been deleted, it stops.

FAQs

Why does a workflow show "skipped"? The reason is recorded. The common ones are: no email address, no mobile number, no marketing consent for that channel, no app installed, or the sending service is not connected.

Can I make a workflow send to everyone, ignoring consent? No. The type of message is fixed by the trigger and cannot be changed when writing it. This is deliberate and protects your gym under the Spam Act.

How is this different from Marketing? Workflows are automatic and triggered by something happening to one person. Marketing is a campaign you send to a list at a time you choose. See Marketing.

A member says they got the same message twice. Each workflow only sends once per person for each event (a message already sent for that person and event is skipped). Check whether two workflows (for example a system one and one you created) both match.

Why did no nudge go out to someone who has not been in for weeks? Nudges only go at the moment someone moves into High risk or Non attender. If they were already there before the workflow was switched on, they do not get one.

How do I stop all automatic texts? Set each text-sending workflow to Paused, or untick Text as a channel.
