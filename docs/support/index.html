Support

What it is for

Support is how you get help from the Helivos team. It has two parts: a help library with answers to common questions, and a chat with real people at Helivos. Use the library first for a quick answer, and the chat for anything specific to your gym.

Who can see it

Everyone on staff. It sits near the bottom of the sidebar above Settings. The same conversation also appears as the Support tab inside the Inbox.

How to use it

Find an answer

  1. Open Support from the sidebar.
  2. On the left is the FAQ, grouped by topic.
  3. Click a question to open the answer. Some answers have a button that takes you straight to the right page, such as Open Getting started.

Start a chat

  1. Open Support and click Start chat.
  2. Describe the problem. Include as much detail as you can: who it affects, what you expected, what happened, what you were doing, and the exact wording of any error.
  3. Send your message. You will get an automatic note straight away explaining that real people reply, not an automated assistant, so the answer will not be instant.
  4. When the team replies, you are notified. The conversation stays open so you can carry on in the same place.

Check on a chat

The list of conversations shows each one's status:

Status Meaning
Open Waiting for the team or in progress.
On hold Parked for now. The conversation shows a linked issue if the team has raised one.
Closed Resolved. You can start a new conversation any time.

You can filter by status and by who wrote last (you or Helivos), and sort by latest or oldest.

Close a conversation

Click Close case when you are happy it is sorted. If you start another chat later, it opens as a new conversation.

FAQs

How quickly will I get a reply? Replies come from the Helivos support team and not from an automated assistant, so they are not instant. You will be notified when someone replies.

Where do I change my plan with Helivos? Settings > Billing shows your plan and tells you to visit Support for changes. Start a chat and say what you would like.

Can my members contact Helivos here? No. Members have their own support screen in the member app that goes to your staff, in the Inbox under Messages. This Support page is for you.

I closed a case by mistake. Start a new conversation and mention the earlier one.
