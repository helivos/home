Home dashboard

The Home dashboard is not a sidebar link, but it is the first page everyone lands on and you can get back to it by clicking your gym's name or logo at the top of the sidebar. It is where most of the money numbers live, so it is included here.

What it is for

A quick read on how the gym is doing today: how much recurring revenue you have, who owes money, who might leave, what is on in the classroom, and who needs a message.

Who can see it

Everyone on staff. Revenue figures are hidden from the Staff role, and some cards are limited to admins and managers.

The dashboard has three tabs.

  • Overview. Billing cards, revenue, sales breakdown, today's classes, new leads and monthly KPIs.
  • Retention. Keep an eye on, today's class focus, congratulate, milestones coming up, and at-risk members.
  • Marketing. What you spend to win members.

How to use it

  1. Open the dashboard and stay on Overview.
  2. Read the billing cards across the top (described below). The small percentage on each card is how it has moved compared with about a month ago.
  3. Use the time period dropdown (Today, 7 days, Month to date, Month) to change what the revenue, new members and similar cards show.
  4. Hover over the small info icon on any card to see what it counts.
  5. Open the Retention tab for the people side: who to keep an eye on and who to congratulate.
  6. If you see a number that looks wrong, check the alert thresholds in Settings > Retention before assuming the data is wrong.

Set your billing alerts

The bell icon in the top bar shows alerts. You choose when they appear.

  1. Open Settings > Retention (admins and managers).
  2. Under Retention thresholds, set:
  3. On-hold member count. Alert when this many members are on hold.
  4. Money owed revenue risk (%). Alert when money owed reaches this share of your paying revenue. 0 turns it off.
  5. Minimum monthly revenue (%). Alert when paying revenue falls below this share of total possible revenue. 0 turns it off.
  6. Churn risk window (days). How many days without a booking before a paying member counts as At Risk.
  7. Click Save changes.

The billing cards, explained

All dollar figures on these cards are monthly equivalents of plan prices. They show what you expect to bill, not cash received. A weekly plan is converted to a monthly figure, a yearly plan is divided by 12, and so on.

Card What it counts
MRR Members with an active, currently billing subscription. The dollar figure is their combined monthly plan value.
Leads Open leads (not won or lost), including leads on a trial. Leads have no plan yet, so each one is valued at the average monthly price of your active plans.
On Hold Members a staff member has put on hold. Their billing is paused, so no charges happen while they are on hold.
Money Owed Members whose subscription is past due, unpaid, or paused outside a staff hold. These are payments you expected and have not received.
At Risk (Churn) Paying members who have not booked a class in the last 14 days (adjustable). Members on hold and members already in Money Owed are not counted here.
Other Sales One-off paid charges this month that are not part of a membership, for example merchandise.
Visits The number of classes members were checked into this month.

Every member lands in exactly one billing group. A member on hold is always counted as On Hold, whatever Stripe says. A member on one of your free trials counts as trialing. Otherwise their Stripe subscription status decides.

A worked example of the alerts

Your paying revenue is $20,000 a month. Members who owe money are worth $2,000 a month, and members with an incomplete sign-up are worth $1,000 a month.

  • Money owed revenue risk set to 8%. 8% of $20,000 is $1,600. Money owed ($2,000) is above that, so you get an alert.
  • Minimum monthly revenue set to 75%. Total possible revenue is paying + owed + incomplete, so $23,000. 75% of that is $17,250. Paying revenue ($20,000) is above it, so no alert. If paying revenue fell to $17,000, you would be alerted.
  • On-hold count set to 5. You get an alert the moment 5 or more members are on hold.

The first two alerts are off until you give them a number above 0.

FAQs

MRR says $18,400 but my bank shows less. Why? MRR is what you expect to bill each month from members with a healthy subscription. It is not cash. The Revenue card shows what was actually paid.

Why does At Risk (Churn) show a different number from High Risk on the Retention page? They measure different things. At Risk (Churn) is about bookings and money. High Risk on the Retention page is about check-ins and each member's own habit. A member can be in one and not the other.

Why is a member counted as At Risk when they came last week? The At Risk card looks at when they last booked a class, not when they last attended. So a member who comes regularly but whose last booking was more than 14 days ago (for example, someone who always walks in) can show here.

What does the percentage change on a card mean? It compares today's figure with a snapshot taken at least 30 days ago. If the gym is newer than 30 days it compares with the oldest snapshot available. If there is no earlier snapshot, or the earlier figure was zero, no percentage shows.

Why do I not see revenue? Revenue and some other cards are for admins and managers. Staff accounts do not see them.

What are the Retention tab's "Keep an eye on" lists? Members who moved into a higher risk level overnight, and members who have stayed at Medium. See Retention for how the levels are worked out.
